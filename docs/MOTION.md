# Follow the source — component film

The film turns one investigation into a sequence of actions and consequences: a passing steady-state test becomes a question, its citation opens original lines, retained versions expose a changed threshold, and a retrieval assay keeps one missed source visible. The audience is an engineering reviewer or recruiter seeing the project for the first time.

The 32-second landscape composition is 1920 × 1080 at 60 fps. Its visual world shares the application's paper, olive, rust and typography. A drawn thread connects the evidence; source panels move into reading positions instead of arriving as whole browser screenshots. The closing mark resolves the same collection identity.

## Sequence

| Global time | Development |

| 0–8 s | The case file unfolds into the load-test and incident documents. Their 118 ms and 12 s dispatch measurements lead to the investigation question. |
| 7–17 s | The actual citation token rises in the clear lane between the answer and source panes. The highlighted cause gives way to the corrective decision at line 12, with a modest inspection zoom and a reading hold. |

| 24–32 s | The twelve-question assay develops beside the fixture metrics. Q11 retains its missing source. The evidence recedes into the mark, closing phrase and thread. |

The one-second handoffs overlap the scenes. Slide and wipe transitions use a controlled easing curve. All motion derives from the Remotion frame; web transitions and wall-clock timers are disabled in the film surface.

## Shared source

The composition imports the application's `AnswerText`, `CitationToken`, `SourceLines`, `VersionPane`, `Metric`, `RetrievalAssay`, `EvidenceTile` and `AtlasMark`. Comparison uses the same changed-line calculation as the app. Film props contain only authored fictional documents and the checked-in evaluation results. The separate operator-handoff example changes a queue threshold from 10,000 to 8,000 events; it is outside the eight-source evaluation corpus.

Local font files carry the notices in [THIRD-PARTY.md](../THIRD-PARTY.md). The stereo score is procedural and authored for the showcase. It contains no narration or recorded personal audio.
